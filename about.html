About This Space

This site is an antidote to algorithmic feeds, endless scrolling, and modern web clutter. Designed with a book-like aesthetic, it serves as a space for slow thoughts, personal reflections, and deliberate focus.

I am a software engineering student writing about reclaiming attention, unlearning digital habits, and returning to deep work and physical books.

There are no analytics tracking your visit, no advertisements, and no pop-ups. If you wish to join the conversation or suggest a book, feel free to drop a note in the guestbook on the home page.

— Written from a quiet desk.
