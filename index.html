On Doomscrolling and Reclaiming Focus

Published September 2026 • 2 min read

Hey, is there anyone into reading books?

Like, not recently— long time ago.

I've found that binge-watching anime is no longer entertaining to me.

All interests have been destroyed by doomscrolling and AI slop.

I am not trying to endorse/promote anything like anti-something, following upside-down internet trends, etc.

This is based on my experience as a full-time software engineering student at university, where I have gone through rough times, either financially or socially, and felt insecure. My most significant behaviour since the global lockdown has been doomscrolling.

"Doomscrolling appears as a vicious cycle in which users find themselves get stuck in a pattern of seeking negative information no matter how bad the news is."

Why doomscrolling? Is that bad? It is a big YES. In my opinion, it has impacted my productivity, such as sharpening my coding skills and critical thinking. I can quote a fact from a research paper where doomscrolling appears as a vicious cycle in which users find themselves get stuck in a pattern of seeking negative information no matter how bad the news is (Satici et al., 2023).

The point is, I am writing this post for my own well-being and also to indirectly bring awareness to the public. Therefore, I used to read books every week since middle school and . Anyway, that piece of brick is annoying - smartphone.

If you have read something in your free time, let me know in comments - I kind of clueless here about which title I should read.

Ciao.

Reference

Satici, S. A., Gocet Tekin, E., Deniz, M. E., & Satici, B. (2022). Doomscrolling Scale: its Association with Personality Traits, Psychological Distress, Social Media Use, and Wellbeing. Applied Research in Quality of Life, 18(2), 833–847. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11482-022-10110-7
